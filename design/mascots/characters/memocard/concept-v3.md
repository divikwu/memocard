# MemoCard concept v3

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Revised from concept v2 toward a calm, quietly witty note-taking companion: smaller eyes and highlights, slimmer round glasses, no blush, fine matte felt, restrained smile, simpler bow tie, no shoulder pedestal. This is a single design concept for review.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE refined brand mascot concept, actual transparent PNG.
The attached image is the previous MemoCard mascot design. REGENERATE its design towards a mature, quietly witty, thoughtful note-taking companion for an adult productivity app. Retain recognizable identity: golden-yellow rounded-square sticky-note slab, a gently curled upper-right corner, black circular glasses, and a simple small black bow tie attached directly to its lower edge. Single complete front-facing character centered with generous transparent margin, square canvas. No separate shoulders, neck, arms, hands, legs or pedestal.
Make a clear, deliberate change in personality and proportions:
- Eyes MUCH SMALLER than reference, approximately 45-50% of the reference pupil dimensions. Small calm dark ovals with a subtle relaxed upper eyelid, placed naturally behind the glasses. Avoid wide-eyed surprise or large watery baby eyes. No large white catchlights; at most a tiny quiet reflection. Attentive, thoughtful and gently curious, not sleepy, stern or grumpy.
- Round glasses frames substantially slimmer, about half the reference rim thickness, refined matte charcoal black, precise simple circles and understated bridge. Slightly smaller overall glasses relative to note. They should resemble elegant everyday spectacles, not padded toy goggles.
- Mouth is a tiny restrained shallow curved smile, slender matte charcoal line, gently asymmetrical for a hint of wit. No big raised cheeks or pronounced U-shaped grin.
- NO blush, NO pink/orange cheeks, NO eyelashes, NO eyebrows shaped into a cute pleading expression.
- Material is fine dense wool felt or tactile matte stationery, with subtle short fibers and restrained warm diffuse shading. Flat broad face, thin edge about 4-5% of width, a crafted thin note rather than a puffy plush cushion. Avoid heavy inflated volume, fluffy fur, rubber toy gloss, intense yellow glow or oversaturated lemon brightness. Use a warm mustard-gold yellow that still reads clearly as MemoCard.
- Compact rounded-square note silhouette with slightly less rounding than reference and a modest softly curved upper-right fold. Keep elegant softened geometry rather than baby-soft pillow roundness. Face area is clean, absolutely no three horizontal writing lines, marks or text.
- Bow tie smaller and simpler, around 26% note width, matte charcoal felt, modest flat geometric loops and a neat knot. It sits directly on the note's bottom edge, no human body below it.
The overall mood: a calm observant librarian or quiet writer, approachable through subtle expression and thoughtful proportions, composed, intelligent, understated. Reduce the overt kawaii/sweet toy impression strongly while retaining charm. Professional high quality 3D tactile brand character, warm studio diffuse light. No scenery, ground shadow, labels, words, watermark, props or floating icons. Genuinely transparent background, not painted checkerboard. Exactly one character, fills about 70% of canvas.
