## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one polished character refinement concept on actual transparent background.
References: Image 1 is the current MemoCard sprite sheet, use its middle front-facing pose as identity reference. Image 2 is the original MemoCard IP, use it for the approachable personality, yellow tactile plush felt, round black glasses, gentle smile, folded upper-right corner, and black bow tie.
Create ONE single front-facing refined MemoCard character, centered with generous transparent margin, NOT a sprite sheet, no collage or multiple views. Full character visible.
Refinements:
1. The yellow rounded-square sticky-note slab itself is the complete character silhouette. Eliminate the separate human-like shoulder/chest pedestal from image1. Attach the small black bow tie directly to the lower edge of the note, with its top half overlapping the note face slightly. No separate neck or body, no shoulders. The bow tie is part of the note character.
2. Tighten proportions: a compact broad note face, width about 1.08 times its height, with less blank yellow area under the mouth. Keep a rounded-square silhouette, slightly asymmetrical paper softness. Avoid a tall lower chin.
3. Softer attentive eye expression: thick matte-black circular glasses, with dark warm oval eyes about 10-15% smaller than image1 pupils, one restrained white highlight each, subtly relaxed happy upper eye shape. Both eyes OPEN, gentle and approachable, not startled or vacant. Small charming upward smile centered just below the glasses, subtle warm cheek shading, understated blush.
4. Keep a gently curled physical top-right corner, a soft curved paper fold rather than a sharp triangle. The fold should be a recognizable signature, not oversized.
5. Preserve a THIN NOTE CARD structure with a nearly planar broad face and only a narrow softly rounded edge, about 5% head width deep. Tactile golden-yellow plush felt fibers, soft warm diffuse studio lighting, rounded seams and crafted quality, matte black padded glasses/bow tie. Keep material detail readable but not noisy.
Absolutely no three horizontal writing lines or text on the note; the face is clean yellow. No arms, hands, legs, pencil, extra accessories, floating symbols or background. No ground shadow, no labels, no watermark. The character fills around 72% of square canvas, crisp silhouette, actual alpha transparency, not painted checkerboard. Attractive professional brand mascot, simple memorable outline, warm and friendly rather than robotic.
